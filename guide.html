Good to Know

Nearest airport
Cochin International Airport (COK), at Nedumbassery, north of the city.
Getting around
App cabs such as Uber and Ola work across Kochi, and auto-rickshaws are everywhere for short hops. The Kochi Metro runs through the city, and the Water Metro boats are the pleasantest way across to Fort Kochi.
January weather
Warm and mostly dry — light cottons by day, and it stays pleasant in the evening.
Need help?
Ask either family — we would be glad to arrange a pick-up or point you the right way.
