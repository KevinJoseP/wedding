Our Story

It began, as the best stories do, with a word in the right ear — a family known to a family, a photograph passed across a sitting room, and two names weighed together over many cups of tea. On a bright January morning in 2026 came the pennukanal: Kevin and his people called at Maliyil House, Alappuzha, the tea went quite cold, and by the time the plates were cleared both households already knew what God had long since decided.

Kevin, an engineer with an old soul, and Treesa, a doctor who can silence a room with a hymn, had their match fixed by the elders that September. This November, before the altar of St. Michael’s, Thathampally, they give their word — and in January, what was two families’ friendship becomes, by grace, one household.
